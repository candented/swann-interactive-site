How it works

Technology working quietly in the background while your business keeps moving.

Here’s how an opportunity moves through the system — and how we build it around your business.

Book a Revenue Capture Audit

The system

Eight stages. Every opportunity gets a next step.

  1. 01Lead
  2. 02Answer
  3. 03Qualify
  4. 04Book
  5. 05Follow Up
  6. 06Reactivate
  7. 07Retain
  1. STAGE 01

    Capture

    Calls, forms, ads, web inquiries, texts, old leads and customer lists enter one system.

  2. STAGE 02

    Respond

    An immediate response by voice, SMS or email — whichever fits the moment.

  3. STAGE 03

    Qualify

    The system gathers the information your business actually needs.

  4. STAGE 04

    Book

    Qualified opportunities land on the right calendar or go straight to staff.

  5. STAGE 05

    Follow Up

    Prospects who don’t convert right away keep getting appropriate follow-up.

  6. STAGE 06

    Reactivate

    Old leads, past customers, open estimates and missed appointments are contacted again.

  7. STAGE 07

    Retain

    Reminders, rebooking prompts, service notices, review and referral requests.

  8. STAGE 08

    Report

    Calls, conversations, appointments, leads recovered and opportunities influenced.

Working with us

How we build it with you.

Built around your real workflow — not a template you have to adapt to.

  1. STEP 01

    Revenue Capture Audit

    We map how calls, leads, follow-up, booking and your customer database work today, and where opportunities leak out.

  2. STEP 02

    System design

    We design the workflow around your real business: what gets answered, how it qualifies, where it books, who gets escalated and when.

  3. STEP 03

  5. STEP 05

  6. STEP 06

    Report & improve

    Simple reporting on calls, conversations, appointments and recovered leads, plus ongoing tuning.

Common questions

How long does it take to get started?

It depends on scope. A focused starting point, like missed-call recovery, can be set up much faster than a full multi-channel system. The audit gives you a realistic timeline for your situation.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. The goal is to reduce work, not add another dashboard. You’ll receive summaries and alerts where you already work, and we handle the system.

What do you need from my team?

Your knowledge of the business: services, rules, common questions, and how you want different situations handled. We turn that into the system.

What if a customer wants a human?

They get one. Escalation paths — live transfer, urgent text alerts, callback tasks — are part of every system we build.
