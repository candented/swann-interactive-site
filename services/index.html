How it fits together

Eight stages. One always-on system.

  1. STAGE 01

    Capture

    Calls, forms, ads, web inquiries, texts, old leads and customer lists enter one system.

  2. STAGE 02

    Respond

    An immediate response by voice, SMS or email — whichever fits the moment.

  3. STAGE 03

    Qualify

    The system gathers the information your business actually needs.

  4. STAGE 04

    Book

    Qualified opportunities land on the right calendar or go straight to staff.

  5. STAGE 05

    Follow Up

    Prospects who don’t convert right away keep getting appropriate follow-up.

  6. STAGE 06

    Reactivate

    Old leads, past customers, open estimates and missed appointments are contacted again.

  7. STAGE 07

    Retain

    Reminders, rebooking prompts, service notices, review and referral requests.

  8. STAGE 08

    Report

    Calls, conversations, appointments, leads recovered and opportunities influenced.
