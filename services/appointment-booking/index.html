AI Appointment Booking

Turn conversations into calendar appointments automatically.

The moment a customer is ready, they get a time on your calendar — no phone tag, no “someone will call you back.”

Book a Revenue Capture Audit See How It Works

The problem

Interest fades between “I’m interested” and “you’re booked.”

Every step between a customer saying yes and a time on the calendar is a chance to lose them: a callback that comes tomorrow, a voicemail, a back-and-forth about availability.

Booking at the moment of interest — on the call, in the text thread, right after the form — closes that gap.

What changes

  • More inquiries turned into booked appointments
  • Fewer no-shows and last-minute gaps
  • Less office time spent on scheduling back-and-forth
  • Customers booked while they’re still motivated
Book a Revenue Capture Audit

What it does

Conversations become calendar appointments — automatically.

  • Real-time availability

    Reads the calendars you connect and offers times that actually work.

  • Qualifies first

    Confirms service area, job type and any must-haves before a slot is used.

  • Books to the right calendar

    By service, territory, crew or staff member — following your rules.

  • Reschedules and cancels

    Customers can move appointments without a phone call to the office.

  • Confirmations

    Instant confirmation by text and email with the details they need.

  • Reminders that reduce no-shows

    Timed reminders with an easy way to confirm or reschedule.

  • Special cases to staff

    Complex jobs, VIP accounts or unusual requests go to a person.

How it works

From first contact to next step.

  1. 1

    Customer is ready

    On a call, in a text thread or after submitting a form.

  2. 2

    Fit confirmed

    The system checks the details that decide whether and where to book.

  3. 3

    Time offered and booked

    Real availability, confirmed in the same conversation.

  4. 4

    Confirmed and reminded

    Confirmation now, reminders later, easy rescheduling throughout.

A strong fit for

  • Businesses that run on estimates, inspections or consultations
  • Service companies with multiple techs, crews or territories
  • Offices spending hours a week on scheduling calls

Common questions

Which calendars does it work with?

Most common business calendars and many field-service and CRM scheduling tools. We confirm your exact setup during the audit.

Can I control what gets booked?

Completely. You set the job types, service areas, buffers, hours and rules. Anything outside them goes to your team.

What about rescheduling?

Customers can reschedule or cancel by replying to their confirmation or reminder, and the calendar updates automatically.
