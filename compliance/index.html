Responsible automation

Consent-based, business-appropriate automation.

We build systems that help businesses respond to and follow up with people who want to hear from them. We don’t build spam, and we don’t enable unsolicited mass robocalling.

Book a Revenue Capture Audit

  • Consent first

    Outbound calls and marketing texts go to people with a relationship with the business or documented consent. We help design consent capture into forms, calls and texts.

  • TCPA & Do Not Call

    Outbound programs can be configured around TCPA requirements, internal and national Do Not Call lists, calling-hour windows and prompt opt-out handling.

  • SMS opt-in & opt-out

    Clear opt-in language, STOP/HELP keyword handling, sender identification, message-frequency expectations and carrier registration requirements.

  • CAN-SPAM

    Commercial email includes accurate sender information, a physical address, honest subject lines and working unsubscribe handling.

  • Call recording & disclosure

    Recording and AI-disclosure settings can be configured around one-party and all-party consent rules and your own policies.

  • Customer data

    Collect only what’s needed, restrict access, and follow your customer data and privacy policies — including for sensitive industries.

What we will and won’t do

We will build systems that answer inbound calls and messages, follow up with people who contacted the business, remind existing customers about appointments and services, and reach out to opted-in contacts with relevant, honest messages.

We won’t build unsolicited mass robocalling or texting, purchase-list blasting, deceptive messaging, or anything designed to get around consent and opt-out rules.

Shared responsibility

Rules vary by channel, industry and state, and they change. We configure systems around applicable requirements and flag risks we see, but each business remains responsible for its own lists, consent records and message content. Nothing on this site is legal advice, and no system can guarantee compliance on its own — we recommend reviewing your program with qualified counsel.
