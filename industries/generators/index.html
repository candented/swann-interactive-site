What usually gets lost

Where opportunities slip away.

  • Inquiry spikes before and after storms that overwhelm the office
  • High-ticket quotes that stall during a long decision

What one lost opportunity might mean

One lost lead can be a large installation and years of maintenance.

Standby generator projects are significant decisions, and the installer who stays in touch often wins. Each installed system can also mean ongoing maintenance and service visits for years.
