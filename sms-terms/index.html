SMS Terms & Messaging Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Program description

Swann Interactive sends text messages to people who request information, an audit or a consultation, or who otherwise agree to receive texts from us. Messages may include responses to your inquiry, appointment scheduling, confirmations and reminders, and occasional information about our services.

Opting in

You opt in by providing your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts — for example, on one of our forms or by texting us first. Consent to receive marketing texts is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency & cost

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

Opting out & help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You’ll receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

Messaging programs we build for clients

Text programs we build for client businesses are sent on behalf of — and governed by the terms and policies of — those businesses. We configure them with opt-in capture, STOP/HELP handling, sender identification and carrier registration requirements in mind. See Responsible Automation.
