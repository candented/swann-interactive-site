Common questions

What does Swann Interactive actually do?

We build always-on revenue systems for service businesses: AI-powered call answering, appointment booking, missed-call text-back, SMS and email follow-up, and customer reactivation — connected so every opportunity gets a next step.

Is this going to replace my staff?

No. It supports them. The system handles the calls and follow-up that fall through the cracks — after hours, overflow, reminders, old leads — and escalates the conversations that need a person.

Do I have to change my phone number or software?

Usually not. We build around your existing number, calendar and CRM wherever possible. The audit confirms exactly what connects.

Where do you work?

We’re based in Charleston, South Carolina and work closely with Lowcountry businesses — and the systems we build serve growing businesses anywhere.

What is the Revenue Capture Audit?

A focused review of how your business handles calls, response speed, booking, follow-up and your existing customer database — and where opportunities are slipping away. You get a clear picture of what to fix first.
